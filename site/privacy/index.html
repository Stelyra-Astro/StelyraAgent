Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stelyra Agent ("Stelyra," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Stelyra mobile application, related websites, account services, and AI features. Stelyra combines astrology calculations with optional AI-powered interpretation, so some data can remain part of the app workflow while selected information is transmitted when you request an online service such as account sync, purchase validation, or AI analysis.

1. Information we process

Account information

If you sign in with Sign in with Apple, we may receive an Apple-provided user identifier and, depending on the choices you make with Apple, your name or email address. Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your personal email.

Astrology profiles and chart information

You may choose to enter information used to calculate or organize astrology charts, such as names or labels, birth date, birth time, location, relationship context, chart selections, and other profile details. Some calculations may be performed by app components before information is used for AI interpretation.

AI prompts, conversations, and analysis context

When you ask Stelyra to generate AI analysis, we process the prompt you submit and the context required for that request. This may include structured astrology facts, chart placements, aspects, timing information, profile labels, themes, prior messages in the relevant conversation, and other information you deliberately attach to the request.

Purchases, entitlements, and credits

Stelyra may process product identifiers, transaction or entitlement status, credit balances, credit ledger events, and other purchase-related metadata needed to provide paid features. Apple processes your actual App Store payment details; Stelyra does not receive your full payment card number.

Technical and support information

We may process basic technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as app version, request timestamps, service errors, authentication events, and abuse-prevention signals. If you contact support, we process the contents of your message and the contact information you provide.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and maintain your Stelyra account;
  • calculate, save, sync, and display supported astrology context;
  • send the context you select to AI infrastructure and return generated analysis;
  • maintain credit balances, validate eligible purchases, and restore supported entitlements;
  • keep conversations or history available where the app provides those features;
  • diagnose errors, secure the service, prevent abuse, and enforce usage limits;
  • respond to support requests and service communications; and
  • comply with applicable law and protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

3. Service providers and disclosures

Stelyra relies on service providers to operate specific parts of the product. Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by:

  • Apple, for App Store distribution, Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, and related platform services;
  • Supabase, for supported authentication, database, account, balance, and history infrastructure;
  • RevenueCat, for purchase, entitlement, and in-app purchase infrastructure;
  • hosting, network, security, and backend infrastructure providers used to route and operate service requests; and
  • AI infrastructure or model providers used to process a request when you explicitly ask Stelyra for AI-generated analysis.

These providers receive information only as needed for the relevant service. Their handling of information is also subject to their own terms, privacy commitments, and our service configuration.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect Stelyra or our users, investigate fraud or security incidents, or complete a corporate transaction involving the service.

4. AI processing

AI analysis is an online operation. When you request it, the prompt and selected context needed to answer the request may leave your device and be processed through Stelyra's backend and one or more AI service providers. Do not submit information that you do not want processed for that purpose.

Stelyra is designed to provide structured astrology context to the model rather than relying only on generic prompt text. That does not make AI output deterministic or error-free.

5. Purchases and payment data

Purchases made through the Apple App Store are processed by Apple. Stelyra and RevenueCat may receive transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase state, entitlement state, and related metadata required to deliver credits or access. We do not receive your full bank or card credentials from Apple.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account and credit records, meet security and fraud-prevention needs, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal obligations. Different categories may have different retention periods.

Conversation history, saved profiles, or other account content may remain associated with your account until you delete the relevant content, delete your account where that control is available, or ask us to delete eligible information.

7. Deletion and your choices

You can choose what profile information and prompts to submit. Where Stelyra provides in-app account deletion or content deletion controls, you may use those controls directly. You may also contact us at Stelyra-Astro@proton.me to request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible personal information.

Some purchase, fraud-prevention, security, or legal records may need to be retained even after an account deletion request. App Store purchase records are also maintained by Apple under Apple's own policies.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection laws in those locations may differ from your local laws. We use service providers and safeguards appropriate to the services we operate and applicable requirements.

9. Children

Stelyra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Stelyra, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No storage, network, authentication system, or AI service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and you should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Stelyra changes. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date and may provide additional notice where required.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to Stelyra-Astro@proton.me.

For your security, do not include passwords, full payment credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.
