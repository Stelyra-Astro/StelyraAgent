How it works

Calculation and interpretation stay separate.

Stelyra does the deterministic astrology work before asking an AI model to interpret the relevant facts. That separation keeps the role of the model clear.

STEP 01

Choose context

Select or add the person, chart, theme, or question you want to explore.

STEP 02

Calculate facts

Stelyra prepares relevant astrology calculations and structured chart information.

STEP 03

Ask the agent

The AI receives the selected context needed for interpretation and returns a conversational analysis.

Privacy by scope

Only send what the analysis needs.

Chart computation and AI interpretation are different parts of the system. When you request AI analysis, selected structured chart facts, prompts, and related context may be sent to the services needed to answer your request.

PurchasesApple handles payment information. Stelyra uses purchase and entitlement information to maintain credits and access.
Account syncSigned-in accounts can store service data needed to keep balances and supported history available across devices.
AI requestsPrompts and selected context are processed by backend and AI infrastructure when you choose to run an analysis.
