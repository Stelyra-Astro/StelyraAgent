# How to choose Life Areas

**Life Areas tell StelyraAgent what each option is mainly about.**

If all options are mainly about the same part of life, choose a **Shared Primary Area**. For three job offers, for example, use **Career & Public Role** once. You do not need to select Career again for every option.

If one option involves something extra, add it under **Additional Life Areas**:

- **Option A — Job in another country:** Travel, Study & Beliefs
- **Option B — Local job:** None

Career remains the main area for both options.

## When each option needs its own Primary Area

If the options are mainly about different parts of life, do not use a Shared Primary Area:

- **Take a new job:** Career & Public Role
- **Study abroad:** Travel, Study & Beliefs
- **Buy a home:** Home & Family

Choose the **Primary Life Area** separately for each option. You can still add **Shared Related Areas** when something, such as money, matters to every option.

## Simple rule

- **Same kind of options:** use one Shared Primary Area.
- **Different kinds of options:** choose a Primary Area for each option.
- **Extra factors for one option:** add Additional Life Areas.
